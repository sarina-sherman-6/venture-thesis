How I evaluate a consumer company

The same four questions run through every thesis, shaped by running Amazon and Walmart PPC and building a food brand from a cold pitch.

Habit

Does the product earn a daily or weekly spot? I look at 90-day repeat and subscription retention before topline growth.

Distribution edge

Is there a channel the brand owns, from venues and GLP-1 prescribers to Amazon rank and retail velocity per store?

Proof

Clinical data, regulatory standing, or validated accuracy that a fast follower cannot copy.

Exit path

Which strategic needs this brand, and are they already investing in the category?
